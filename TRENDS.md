# Market Trends — What the Product Must Account For
*Last updated: June 24, 2026. Re-verify quarterly — this market moves fast.*

This document records current market reality so product decisions are grounded in what's
actually happening, not assumptions. Each trend has a **product implication** — a concrete
rule or feature direction. Sources are dated; flag and re-check anything older than 2025.

---

## 1. The authenticity backlash against AI content — THE defining constraint

**The trend:** Consumer preference for AI-generated creator content collapsed — only **26%**
of consumers prefer AI creator content in 2026, down from 60% in 2023 (Digiday, Jan 14 2026).
Aerie's "no AI… ever" pledge became its most-liked Instagram post ever and tracked with
+23% Q4 sales (Marketing Brew, Mar 30 2026). Platforms now **label and penalize** AI content:
- Instagram "AI Creator" labels rolled out **May 4, 2026** (Social Media Today)
- TikTok auto-labels AI-generated content
- YouTube mandatory AI disclosure (enforced May 2025) — undisclosed AI content **can lose
  ad revenue**

**Product implication (critical):** Our social/content skill must make reps *faster and more
authentic*, never produce detectable "AI slop." Concretely:
- Generate **drafts and talking points the rep personalizes**, not finished posts to paste.
- Favor raw, personal, phone-shot framing over polished copy. Output should read like the
  rep wrote it, because the rep should finish it.
- Never auto-post. Always rep-in-the-loop.
- In-app guidance should *coach authenticity* ("post this in your own words, add your photo")
  — turning a platform risk into a selling point: we keep you compliant AND real.

This is the single most important trend for a rep AI tool in 2026. Build around it.

## 2. FTC is now prosecuting INDIVIDUAL reps for income claims

**The trend:** Enforcement moved from companies to individuals. FTC actions in 2026:
- **Stormy Wellington** — action for unsubstantiated income/lifestyle claims (Apr 2026)
- **IM Mastery Academy** — **$795.8M judgment** (May 2026)
- r/antiMLM holds steady at ~853K members (not fading) — public scrutiny is permanent

**Product implication:** This is direct validation of our 58-pattern income-claim filter —
it is now a *liability shield for the individual rep*, not just a nicety. Lead with it in
marketing: "We keep you on the right side of the FTC." Never weaken the filter. Consider
surfacing *why* a phrase was blocked so reps learn the rule, not just hit a wall.

## 3. Live selling is where the growth is

**The trend:** Livestream shopping is ~22% of US TikTok beauty sales; live sellers saw
**+84% sales YoY**; live converts at **5–12%** vs 2–4% for standard posts
(McKinsey State of Beauty 2026; beautymatter.com Mar 2026). Beauty is the #1 category on
TikTok Shop (US GMV ~$23.4B; US social commerce passed $100B in 2026).

**Product implication:** A **live-selling assistant** is a high-value roadmap item — real-time
talking points, objection responses, and on-the-fly compliance checking during a live. This
is differentiated and maps to where reps are actually growing revenue. (On ROADMAP as 1.3.0+.)

## 4. Platform/marketplace rules constrain WHERE reps can sell

**The trend:** TikTok's US divestiture closed **Jan 22, 2026** (ban averted). But:
- **Mary Kay prohibits third-party-marketplace selling** (Amazon, eBay, and официально
  TikTok Shop) — violating it risks termination. Reps post organically and drive to their
  personal MK site.
- Avon channels sales through its own e-store, not third-party marketplaces.

**Product implication:** Our content skill helps reps **build organic presence and drive to
their official channel** — never coach selling through a prohibited marketplace. The social
skill should generate content that drives DMs / link clicks to the rep's official site, which
is exactly what the compensation plan rewards. Encode this so we never put a rep's account
at risk.

## 5. Gen Z avoids classic MLM but responds to authentic UGC + hybrid affiliate

**The trend:** Younger people avoid traditional downline-recruiting MLM globally (18–24 =
6.9% of direct sellers; 55+ = 28%, WFDSA STATS 2024). BUT Mary Kay's Gen Z push worked —
~30% of 2024 new consultants were under 35 (Marketing Dive, Sept 2025). Winners use authentic
user-generated content and hybrid affiliate-style links, not recruitment pitches.

**Product implication:** Skills should bias toward **product storytelling and customer
relationships**, not recruitment scripts. The reps who succeed in 2026 sell products
authentically. Our follow-up writer, social skill, and product Q&A all serve that motion.
De-emphasize recruitment content (which also reduces FTC exposure).

---

## Net: the 2026 thesis for this product

A winning rep AI tool in 2026 is **a compliance-safe authenticity amplifier**, not a content
factory. It makes reps faster at being genuinely themselves, keeps them clear of FTC and
platform penalties, and points them at live selling and organic-to-official-channel motion —
the two places revenue is actually growing. Everything on the roadmap should ladder up to that.
